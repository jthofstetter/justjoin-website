The idea

Most plans don’t fall apart over the idea. They fall apart over who’s coming. JustJoin shows you what’s happening around you right now and who wants in. You don’t have to know anyone. You just have to join.

How it works.

  1. Your feed shows what’s planned around you. You set the radius yourself, and filters for date, time and keywords do the rest.

  2. One tap and you’re in. Before you join, you see who’s organizing, when and where it starts, the age range and how many spots are left.

  3. Every activity has its own group chat. Sort out where to meet, and if you’re running late, give everyone a heads-up.

  4. Nothing that fits? Create your own activity, public for everyone or private for your friends, and invite people right away.

No plan? QuickJoin.

The map around you is split into hexes. Wherever people are up to something, the hex lights up. Tap it, join the group and use the live chat to agree where to meet.

  • You
  • Hex with people, the number shows how many
  • Your range for joining

Busy? The hex splits.

Zoom in and a hex breaks into seven shards. Each shard has its own people and its own chat. That keeps groups small, even when the area is packed.

  • You’re only ever in one group. Switching takes one tap.
  • You can only join hexes near you.

Your shard: four people, one chat.

More than a meetup.

One activity often leads to the next. JustJoin helps you stay in touch with the people you’ve met.

  • Friends

    Find people by name or @username, send friend requests and keep track of your network.

  • Profile with photos

    Show what you’ve been up to and tag who was there. Your status shows whether you’re active right now.

  • Memories

    Every activity leaves an archive: a public gallery and a private collection. Only people who were there can add photos.

  • Chats

    Direct, in groups or per activity. Share an activity to any chat with one tap.

  • Notifications

    New messages and invites arrive as push notifications, even when the app is closed.

  • Your look

    Classic in black and white, or Red with a red accent. The app is available in English and German.

You decide who’s in.

Whoever starts an activity sets the rules. And you decide what others get to see about you.

  • Age range

    Every activity has an age range, for example 18 to 30.

  • Limited spots

    The organizer decides how many people can join.

  • Public or private

    Private activities are only for friends you invite.

  • Private profile

    If you like, only your friends can see your photos, friends and past activities.

  • No exact location

    In QuickJoin, others only see the hex you’re in, not your exact position.

  • Leave anytime

    You can leave activities, groups and hexes at any time, and delete your account right in the settings.

Questions and answers.

Do I need to know people already?

No. That’s exactly what JustJoin is for. You see what’s happening near you and join in, even if you don’t know anyone there yet.

What’s the difference between an activity and QuickJoin?

An activity is planned, with a place, a time and a description. QuickJoin is spontaneous: you join the people in your hex who are up to something right now and sort out the details in the live chat.

Can others see exactly where I am?

No. In QuickJoin, your location is matched to a hex. Others see the hex and how many people are in it, but not your exact position.

Which phones will JustJoin run on?

iPhone and Android. Both apps are being built right now.

When can I download the app?

As soon as it’s ready. Join the waitlist and you’ll be the first to know.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, at any time. You’ll find it in the settings under Account & Privacy.
